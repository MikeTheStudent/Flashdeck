
de | of / from / about
Sou de Portugal | I am from Portugal
um copo de água | a glass of water
a aula de português | the Portuguese class
perto de casa | near home
longe de aqui | far from here
gostar de + infinitivo | to like doing something
ter de + infinitivo | to have to do something
de manhã | in the morning
de tarde | in the afternoon
de noite | at night
de semana em semana | from week to week


em | in / on / at
em casa | at home
em Portugal | in Portugal
em Lisboa | in Lisbon
no (em + o) | in the / at the (masculine singular)
na (em + a) | in the / at the (feminine singular)
nos (em + os) | in the / at the (masculine plural)
nas (em + as) | in the / at the (feminine plural)
no Porto | in Porto
na cozinha | in the kitchen
nos jardins | in the gardens
nas ruas | in the streets
em cima de | on top of
em baixo de | under / below


para | for / to / in order to
para casa | home (direction)
para o trabalho | to work
para mim | for me
para ti | for you
para ele / ela | for him / her
para nós | for us
Estudo para aprender | I study in order to learn
Este prato é para ti | This dish is for you

de + o = do | of the / from the (masc. sing.)
de + a = da | of the / from the (fem. sing.)
de + os = dos | of the / from the (masc. pl.)
de + as = das | of the / from the (fem. pl.)
do Porto | from Porto
da cozinha | from the kitchen
dos alunos | of the students
das aulas | of the classes


em + o = no | in the (masc. sing.)
em + a = na | in the (fem. sing.)
em + os = nos | in the (masc. pl.)
em + as = nas | in the (fem. pl.)
no frigorífico | in the fridge
na bancada | on the counter
nos pratos | on the plates
nas gavetas | in the drawers

Há (haver) = there is / there are (existence) | Tem (ter) = has / have (possession)
No Porto há um aeroporto | In Porto there is an airport (existence)
O Porto tem um aeroporto | Porto has an airport (possession)
Há leite no frigorífico | There is milk in the fridge
O frigorífico tem leite | The fridge has milk
Há muito trânsito hoje | There is a lot of traffic today
Há estudantes na aula | There are students in the class
Há + noun (no subject) | haver never agrees with the noun — always há
Tem / têm + noun (with subject) | ter agrees with the subject


As mãos estão sujas | The hands are dirty (STATE — resultado)
Ela suja as mãos no jardim | She dirties her hands in the garden (ACTION)
A cozinha está limpa | The kitchen is clean (STATE)
Eles limpam a cozinha | They clean the kitchen (ACTION)
O quarto está arrumado | The room is tidy (STATE)
Ela arruma o quarto | She tidies the room (ACTION)
A janela está fechada | The window is closed (STATE)
Ele fecha a janela | He closes the window (ACTION)
A luz está apagada | The light is off (STATE)
Ela apaga a luz | She turns off the light (ACTION)
A televisão está ligada | The TV is on (STATE)
Ele liga a televisão | He turns on the TV (ACTION)
A sopa está quente / aquecida | The soup is warm (STATE)
Ela aquece a sopa | She heats the soup (ACTION)
A bebida está fria / arrefecida | The drink is cold (STATE)
Ele arrefece a bebida | He cools the drink (ACTION)
O copo está vazio | The glass is empty (STATE)
Ela esvazia o copo | She empties the glass (ACTION)


sujo/a (adj) → sujar (verb) | dirty → to dirty / make dirty
limpo/a (adj) → limpar (verb) | clean → to clean
arrumado/a (adj) → arrumar (verb) | tidy → to tidy
fechado/a (adj) → fechar (verb) | closed → to close
apagado/a (adj) → apagar (verb) | off/erased → to turn off / erase
ligado/a (adj) → ligar (verb) | on/connected → to turn on / connect
aquecido/a (adj) → aquecer (verb) | warm/heated → to heat
arrefecido/a (adj) → arrefecer (verb) | cooled → to cool down
vazio/a (adj) → esvaziar (verb) | empty → to empty


ter de + infinitivo | to have to do something (obligation)
Tenho de estudar | I have to study
Tens de comer | You have to eat
Ele tem de trabalhar | He has to work
Temos de limpar a cozinha | We have to clean the kitchen
Têm de fechar a janela | They have to close the window
Tens de arrumar o quarto | You have to tidy the room


gostar de + infinitivo | to like doing something
gostar de + nome | to like something (noun)
Gosto de cozinhar | I like cooking
Gostas de música? | Do you like music?
Ela gosta de ler | She likes reading
Não gosto de acordar cedo | I don't like waking up early
Gostamos de português | We like Portuguese
Gostam de legumes? | Do they like vegetables?
gostar de + nome → de contracts with article | gostar + do / da / dos / das
Gosto do café | I like the coffee (gostar + de + o)
Gosto da sopa | I like the soup (gostar + de + a)
Não gosto dos cogumelos | I don't like the mushrooms


com | with
com alguém | with someone
com uma coisa | with something
Vou ao cinema com a Maria | I'm going to the cinema with Maria
Faço o trabalho com o meu colega | I do the work with my colleague
Corto o pão com a faca | I cut the bread with the knife
Limpo a mesa com a esponja | I clean the table with the sponge
Bebo café com leite | I drink coffee with milk
com + o = com o (no contraction!) | with the — com does NOT contract with articles
